# Status: public official source index; not a substitute for signed Barangay ordinances
# Last checked: 2026-09-19

SCOPE
The target community is Tandang Sora Poblacion, City of Butuan, Agusan del Norte. The Philippine Statistics Authority lists Tandang Sora Pob. as an urban barangay of Butuan City. Publicly available citywide laws are issued by the Sangguniang Panlungsod ng Butuan. A citywide code may apply in Tandang Sora, but it must not be described as a Barangay Tandang Sora ordinance unless a Barangay-approved record says so.

ANSWERING POLICY
1. Cite the official Sangguniang Panlungsod catalog when summarizing these citywide codes.
2. Do not invent a curfew hour, noise cutoff, waste collection schedule, fine, penalty, fee, or effective date from this index alone.
3. Do not claim that a citywide code is a Barangay Tandang Sora ordinance.
4. For a Barangay-specific rule, say: “Kailangan ko munang kumpirmahin ito sa Barangay Tandang Sora Administrator dahil wala akong nakitang signed public Barangay record para sa partikular na rule.”
5. The Barangay must upload signed and current copies of its ordinances, resolutions, Citizen’s Charter, and effectivity/repeal records before the assistant presents them as Barangay-specific law.

REQUIRED BARANGAY RECORDS
- Current approved Barangay ordinances and resolutions.
- Curfew, noise, waste, parking, business, public safety, and community conduct rules.
- Ordinance number, title, approval/effectivity date, covered area, penalties, amendments, and repeal status.
- Signed approval or publication record and the Barangay Administrator’s review date.
