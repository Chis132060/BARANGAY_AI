## What is a Certificate of Indigency?
A Certificate of Indigency is an official document certifying that a resident belongs to an indigent or low-income family. It is commonly used to:
- Avail of free or discounted medical services at government hospitals
- Apply for scholarship programs (CHED, DOST, LGU scholarships)
- Qualify for government assistance programs (4Ps, DSWD Ayuda)
- Support legal aid applications

## Requirements
1. Valid Government-Issued ID or Barangay ID
2. Letter of Purpose (a simple letter stating why you need the certificate)
3. Proof of Residency in the Barangay (if not yet on record)

## Processing Fee
The Certificate of Indigency is issued FREE OF CHARGE. No processing fee is required.

## Who Qualifies?
A resident qualifies for a Certificate of Indigency if their household monthly income is at or below ₱10,481 (as per the Philippine poverty threshold for a family of five). The Barangay Secretary may verify this through a short interview.

## Processing Time
- Standard: Issued on the same day if all requirements are complete and submitted before 3:00 PM.

## Important Notes
- This certificate is valid for 3 months from the date of issuance.
- Falsifying information to obtain this certificate is punishable under applicable laws.
- The Barangay Captain or authorized official must sign and seal the document.
