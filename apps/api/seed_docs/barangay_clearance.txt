# Barangay Clearance — Official Policy Document
# Barangay Tandang Sora, Butuan City
# Effective: January 2024

## What is a Barangay Clearance?
A Barangay Clearance is an official document issued by the Barangay that certifies that the requesting resident has no pending case, complaint, or violation filed against them in the Barangay. It is commonly required for job applications, business permits, and other legal transactions.

## Requirements for Barangay Clearance
To apply for a Barangay Clearance, residents must bring the following:
1. Valid Government-Issued ID (any one of the following):
   - PhilSys National ID
   - Driver's License
   - Passport
   - Voter's ID
   - SSS / GSIS ID
2. Proof of Residency (if name is not yet in the barangay records):
   - Utility bill (electricity, water) showing your address
   - Lease/rental contract
3. Completed Application Form (available at the Barangay Hall front desk)

## Processing Fee
The standard processing fee for a Barangay Clearance is ₱50.00.
Senior Citizens and Persons with Disability (PWD) are entitled to a 20% discount per applicable law.

## Processing Time
- Standard: 1 to 2 working days
- Rush (additional ₱30): Same-day release if submitted before 10:00 AM

## Office Hours
The Barangay Hall is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Closed on weekends and public holidays.

## How to Apply
1. Go to the Barangay Hall and proceed to the Document Request window.
2. Fill out the Barangay Clearance Request Form.
3. Submit your valid ID and requirements.
4. Pay the processing fee at the cashier.
5. Return on the scheduled release date to claim your document.
