## Referral guidance for residents without food or money

1. **Urgent today:** Go or ask for help at the **Barangay Tandang Sora Hall** and tell staff if the household has no food today. Ask for urgent social-worker referral and whether the receiving office needs a Certificate of Indigency or other certification.
2. **City support:** Ask the **Butuan City Social Welfare and Development Department (CSWDD)** for assessment. The city has multiple district offices, so staff must confirm which one handles Tandang Sora before giving a particular district address.
3. **Regional assistance:** Residents may seek an AICS assessment at **DSWD Caraga Crisis Intervention Section**, DSWD Field Office, R. Palma Street, Barangay Dagohoy, Butuan City. Ask about food assistance and urgent financial/material support.
4. **What to bring:** Any valid ID if available, and supporting documents for the particular need. If no ID, ask a social worker whether a barangay identity certification can be accepted. Additional evidence depends on the service.
5. **Do not promise:** automatic approval, a fixed assistance amount, food packs, availability on a specific date, fixed queue time, a specific district office for Tandang Sora, or a barangay-issued ordinance number.

This is a government-services referral, **not** a new resident document request type, permit, income assistance application, or a guarantee of aid. Ate Sora's generated Admin PDF summarizes these database records and links to the original official PDF.
