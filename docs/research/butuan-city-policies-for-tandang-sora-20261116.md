# Butuan City ordinances relevant to Barangay Tandang Sora — verified source register

Research date: 2026-11-16. This is an evidence and data-entry register, not an enacted barangay ordinance.

- [Official Butuan City Government July 8, 2026 dumping enforcement report](https://www.butuan.gov.ph/%F0%9D%97%9C%F0%9D%97%9F%F0%9D%97%98%F0%9D%97%9A%F0%9D%97%94%F0%9D%97%9F-%F0%9D%97%A1%F0%9D%97%9A%F0%9D%97%94-%F0%9D%97%97%F0%9D%97%A8%F0%9D%97%A0%F0%9D%97%A3%F0%9D%97%9C%F0%9D%97%A1%F0%9D%97%9A/) cites **SP Ordinance 5445-2017 (Anti-Illegal Dumping)** and **City Ordinance 7192-2024 (Ecological Solid Waste Code)** as the operative bases for reporting and enforcement against illegal dumping.

- [2022 Butuan City Ecological Statistics Yearbook PDF](https://www.butuan.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Approved%20Butuan%20City%20Ecological%20Statistics%20Yearbook%20202266fb43e1a7374.pdf) lists **Tandang Sora MRF in Purok 6**, with alternate-daily residual and biodegradable waste pickup *as reported for 2022*. This is historical data; never present it as a current 2026 schedule without confirmation.

## Current verified scope

All the ordinance numbers above are **Butuan City-wide**, which may apply to residents of Barangay Tandang Sora. No authenticated, signed Tandang Sora-specific barangay ordinance was located in the reviewed public record or production policy registry. The production registry originally contained only two unverified Draft entries. Eight citywide source-index summaries were therefore added with status **Under Review**, NOT Active, with precise ordinance identifiers, jurisdiction, issuer and public government source URLs.

Citywide, government-published reference summaries are independently published in `verified_legal_sources`, which resident Ate Sora can cite. The detailed 5445-2017, 7192-2024 and RA 9003 garbage-handling guidance is source-gated, clearly scoped, and has no guessed penalties, fines, or collection schedules.

## What residents should do about dumping

1. Segregate household waste and use authorized municipal or barangay waste collection arrangements; ask the current collection schedule at Barangay Tandang Sora Hall or City ENRO.
2. If someone is dumping garbage illegally, document the location and details safely without confrontation or disclosing identifiable information publicly.
3. Report to Barangay Tandang Sora officials and seek referral to the Butuan City Environment and Natural Resources Department (CENRD) if unresolved or involving hazardous/large deposits.
4. For an exact fine, offense classification or procedural deadline, consult the **signed ordinance and latest amendments** with the city council or responsible enforcement office.

## Required originals for full local-policy publication

The Sangguniang Barangay must provide its own ordinance book or signed certified ordinance PDFs and amendments to create real Tandang Sora-issued `Active` policies. For citywide ordinances, city council original scanned or signed PDF should be attached and reviewed before upgrading a Policies registry source-index entry from Under Review to Active.

**Do not infer a barangay ordinance merely from RA 9003 or from a citywide Butuan ordinance. Do not turn summaries into alleged complete original legal texts.**
