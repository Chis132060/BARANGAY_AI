# Barangay AI Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist before enabling Smart Barangay AI for public resident use. A barangay representative must sign off each required item.

## Official information

- [ ] Barangay profile, address, contact numbers, and office hours are verified.
- [ ] Every public service has approved requirements, fees, processing time, and responsible office.
- [ ] Current ordinances, emergency guidance, and announcements have an owner and review date.
- [ ] Superseded documents are archived and excluded from resident retrieval.
- [ ] No document contains unnecessary personal or sensitive resident information.

## AI behavior and safety

- [ ] The AI answers only from approved sources and shows citations when available.
- [ ] The AI clearly says when information is unavailable or needs staff verification.
- [ ] Legal, medical, emergency, and enforcement questions include a human/staff referral.
- [ ] Guest mode cannot access resident records, submit requests, or expose private data.
- [ ] Prompt injection, abusive input, and unsafe output tests have passed.

## Language and accessibility

- [ ] Tagalog, Cebuano/Bisaya, and English responses have been reviewed by fluent staff.
- [ ] Selected language applies to welcome text, chat, clarification, TTS, and voice input.
- [ ] The interface works on mobile screens, slow connections, and low-end devices.
- [ ] Important information is also available as readable text, not audio-only.

## Human handoff

- [ ] Hotline, office location, operating hours, and escalation contact are current.
- [ ] Residents can report an incorrect answer or request staff assistance.
- [ ] Staff know how to review flagged answers and update the source document.
- [ ] Emergency prompts provide the correct emergency contact instead of delaying for AI chat.

## Launch validation

- [ ] At least 50 real resident questions were tested in all supported languages.
- [ ] Accuracy, citation quality, refusal behavior, and response time were recorded.
- [ ] API, database, TTS, backups, monitoring, and rate limits were tested.
- [ ] A rollback owner and incident contact are assigned.
- [ ] Pilot approval and launch date are recorded by the Barangay Administrator.

## Review cadence

Review monthly during the pilot, then quarterly after stable operations. Review immediately after an ordinance, fee, office-hour, leadership, or emergency-contact change.
